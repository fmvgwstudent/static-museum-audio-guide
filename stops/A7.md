### Prayer mat   

> Kashmir (fabric), Iran (compilation), mid-19th century 

> The Textile Museum Collection 2023.17.1 


This piece would've been used as a mat for Muslim prayer
or as a wall hanging. It was made in Kashmir in the mid 19th century, but likely for the Iranian market.

Its fine wool and pashmina weaving makes it an object of very high status. It could have even been a diplomatic gift.
Prayer mats hold great spiritual power in the lives of Muslim people in the past, as well as today.

This object is one of many in <em>Enduring Traditions</em> that features commentary from heritage community members living in and around Washington DC. This piece inspired a woman
to reflect on her own family's prayer mats. She says,

> "the physicality of it to stand up, kneel down, put your head on the mat, requires one
> to concentrate their energy for a few moments every day. I believe that the textile holds that same energy that we put into that process.
> It is like the fibers are holding the breath, the concentration, the attention of the person praying."
