Hello, welcome to the George Washington University Museum and the Textile Museum. My name is Haley Shattuck and I am the Program's Associate
for Public Engagement here at the museum. Since its founding in 1925, The Textile Museum has amassed one of the world's most renowned collections
of handmade textile art with over 21,000 objects spanning five millennia and five continents. Today, I'll be taking you through <em>Enduring Traditions: Celebrating the World of Textiles</em>.

<em>Enduring Traditions</em> is the second of our centennial exhibitions, and it is focused on themes of cultural significance as well
as highlighting the important stories of the people who made and used these objects. I'll be showing you several objects on view in the
exhibition today, and we'll be exploring how they represent themes of power, spirituality, and identity, as well as the significance they hold
for descendant communities today.
