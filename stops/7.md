### Prayer mat   

> Kashmir (fabric), Iran (compilation), mid-19th century 

> The Textile Museum Collection 2023.17.1

This piece could have been used as a mat for Muslim prayer or as a wall hanging. It was
constructed in Kashmir in the mid 19th century but was likely made for the Iranian market.

It’s fine wool and pashmina weaving means it would have been a luxury item for high level society,
possibly even a diplomatic gift. Prayer mats hold great spiritual power and significance in the
lives of Muslim people of the past as well as today.

This piece is one of many in <em>Enduring Traditions</em> that features commentary from heritage community members living in DC today.
This piece inspired a woman to reflect on her own family’s prayer mats:

> “The physicality of it –
> to stand up, kneel down, put your head on the mat – requires one to concentrate their energy
> for a few moments every day. I believe that the textile holds that same energy that we put into
> that process. It is like the fibers are holding the breath, the concentration, the attention of the
> person praying.”
